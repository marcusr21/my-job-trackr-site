Why UK Job Seekers Switch from Huntr

Huntr does a good job of tracking applications in a kanban-style board. But if you're based in the UK, a few things quickly become frustrating:

  • Dollar pricing. Huntr's paid plan is charged in USD, which means you're paying a variable amount in GBP depending on the exchange rate, and your card may charge a foreign transaction fee. My Job Trackr is priced in GBP — £2.99/month, no surprises.
  • No mobile app. Huntr is web-only. My Job Trackr has native iOS and Android apps, so you can log an application or check deadlines from your phone on the go — without opening a browser.
  • No offer comparison. Once you get to the offer stage, Huntr doesn't help you compare them. My Job Trackr lets you lay offers side-by-side so you can make a clear decision.
  • No deadline reminders. You have to manage follow-up timing yourself in Huntr. My Job Trackr sends reminders so you don't miss a deadline or forget to chase a recruiter.

My Job Trackr is free to start — no credit card required. Pro plans are £2.99/month (billed monthly) or available on a free-forever tier with 3 tracked jobs.
