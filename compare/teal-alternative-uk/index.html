Why UK Job Seekers Look for a Teal Alternative

Teal has built a comprehensive career platform — AI resume scoring, LinkedIn optimisation, job matching. But for most UK job seekers, that's a lot more than they need, and a few things stand out as problems:

  • Dollar pricing. Teal charges in USD, which adds currency conversion costs and variability. My Job Trackr charges in GBP at a flat £2.99/month.
  • No mobile app. Teal is web-only. If you want to log an application or check your pipeline from your phone, you need a browser. My Job Trackr has native iOS and Android apps.
  • More product than most people need. Teal bundles job tracking with AI-powered resume tools, career coaching prompts, and LinkedIn optimisation. That's genuinely useful for some people — but if you just want to track where you've applied and when to follow up, it's a lot of noise.
  • US job market focus. Teal's job data and integrations are heavily US-centric. UK job seekers on Reed, Totaljobs, or direct company sites don't benefit from this the same way.

My Job Trackr is free to start — no credit card required. Pro plans are £2.99/month with unlimited tracking, deadline reminders, and full dashboard access.

What My Job Trackr Does Instead

My Job Trackr focuses on one thing: helping you stay on top of your job search. No AI resume scoring, no career coaching upsells — just a clean, fast way to track applications, manage interviews, and compare offers.

  • Log applications in seconds — company, role, date, status
  • Track every stage from applied through to offer or rejection
  • Set deadline reminders so you follow up at the right time
  • Compare offers side-by-side when you reach the final stage
  • Available on web, iOS, and Android

Whether you're tracking a broad job search or specifically managing graduate scheme applications with multiple rounds and deadlines, the focus is on keeping you organised without adding complexity.

Which One Is Right for You?

If you want AI-generated resume suggestions and a full career suite, Teal has more features — but you'll pay in dollars and work without a mobile app. If you want a focused, mobile-friendly job tracker priced in GBP with a free tier, My Job Trackr is the better fit for UK job seekers.
