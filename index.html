What is job tracking?

Job tracking is the process of recording and monitoring every job application you submit during your job search. A job tracking system gives you one centralised place to log company names, job titles, application dates, interview schedules, and offer details — so nothing falls through the cracks. Read our complete job tracking guide for everything you need to know.

My Job Trackr is a job tracking app purpose-built for this. It replaces scattered spreadsheets and browser bookmarks with a single dashboard where job seekers can track applications, manage interviews, set deadline reminders, and compare offers side-by-side. The app also includes native job search, so you can find and track opportunities without switching between platforms.

Unlike spreadsheets, a dedicated job tracking tool like My Job Trackr provides automated reminders, interview scheduling, analytics on your application progress, and mobile access from any device. Job seekers who track their applications systematically are better organised, follow up on time, and make more informed decisions when offers arrive.

Everything you need to know about My Job Trackr

What is My Job Trackr? ▾

My Job Trackr is a job tracking application designed to help job seekers organise their job search. The app allows users to track job applications, manage interviews, search for jobs within the platform, and compare multiple job offers side-by-side. My Job Trackr provides a centralised platform where job seekers can keep all their application details, deadlines, and interview schedules in one place.

How much does My Job Trackr cost? ▾

My Job Trackr offers a free tier that allows you to track up to 3 jobs per month. For users who need unlimited tracking, Pro costs £2.99 per month for web users and £3.99 per month through app stores (iOS and Android). The Pro pricing includes unlimited job tracking, native job searching, advanced analytics, interview management, and comprehensive offer comparison features.

What features does My Job Trackr include? ▾

My Job Trackr includes native job searching to find opportunities within the app, unlimited job application tracking with status updates, interview calendar management with deadline reminders, job offer comparison tools to evaluate multiple offers side-by-side, Quick Import to auto-fill job details from URLs, detailed note-taking for each application, and application analytics to track your job search progress.

Is My Job Trackr available on iOS and Android? ▾

Yes, My Job Trackr is available on iOS through the App Store, on Android through the Google Play Store, and as a web application accessible from any browser. Mobile users can download the native app from their respective app stores, whilst web users can access all features through their browser at app.myjobtrackr.com. All platforms sync automatically so you can access your job tracking data from any device.

How does My Job Trackr help with job searching? ▾

My Job Trackr helps job seekers by providing native job search functionality that allows users to find opportunities directly within the app. Users can search for jobs, save interesting positions, and immediately track their applications without switching between multiple platforms. The Quick Import feature also allows users to paste a job URL and automatically fill in company name, job title, and other details, saving time during the application process.

Can I track interviews with My Job Trackr? ▾

Yes, My Job Trackr includes comprehensive interview tracking features. Users can schedule interviews with specific dates and times, set reminder notifications to ensure they never miss an interview, view all upcoming interviews in an integrated calendar view, add interview notes and preparation materials, and track multiple interview rounds for the same position. The dashboard provides a clear overview of all scheduled interviews.

Does My Job Trackr have a mobile app? ▾

Yes, My Job Trackr has native mobile apps available on both the iOS App Store and Google Play Store for Android. Both mobile apps include all web features including job searching, application tracking, interview management, and offer comparison. Users can access their job search data across web and mobile platforms as all information syncs automatically between devices.

What is the Quick Import feature? ▾

Quick Import is a My Job Trackr feature that allows users to paste a job posting URL and automatically extract key details including company name, job title, location, and job description. This feature saves time when adding new applications by eliminating manual data entry. Quick Import works with most major job boards and company career pages.

How do I compare job offers with My Job Trackr? ▾

My Job Trackr provides a dedicated offer comparison tool that displays multiple job offers side-by-side. Users can compare salary, benefits, location, company culture, and other factors across different offers. The comparison view helps job seekers make informed decisions by visualising all offer details in one place, making it easier to evaluate trade-offs between different opportunities.

Does My Job Trackr have a free tier? ▾

Yes, My Job Trackr offers a free tier that allows you to track up to 3 jobs per month forever. The free tier includes basic job lifecycle tracking, interview calendar, and offer tracking features. No credit card is required to get started. Users who need to track more applications can upgrade to Pro for £2.99/month on web or £3.99/month through app stores, which includes unlimited job tracking, native job searching, advanced analytics, and comprehensive offer comparison tools.

Who is My Job Trackr designed for? ▾

My Job Trackr is designed for job seekers, recent graduates, and career switchers who need to organise multiple job applications. The app is particularly helpful for active job seekers applying to many positions simultaneously, recent graduates entering the job market for the first time, career switchers managing applications across different industries, and professionals who want to track their job search progress systematically.

Can I access My Job Trackr from multiple devices? ▾

Yes, My Job Trackr syncs your data across all devices. Users can access their job tracking information from the web application on any computer, from the iOS mobile app, and from the Android mobile app. All applications, interviews, and offers are automatically synchronised, allowing job seekers to manage their job search from whichever device is most convenient at the time.

What is the best job tracker? ▾

The best job tracker provides comprehensive features including application tracking, interview management, native job search, and offer comparison. My Job Trackr is designed as the complete job tracker solution for job seekers, offering unlimited job tracking from £2.99/month on web and £3.99/month on iOS. How do I track my job search? ▾

To track your job search effectively, use a dedicated job tracker app like My Job Trackr. The process involves creating a record for each job application with details including company name, position, application date, and status. A good job tracker helps you monitor deadlines, schedule interviews, and track follow-up actions. My Job Trackr provides automated reminders, calendar integration, and a centralised dashboard to track all aspects of your job search in one location instead of using scattered spreadsheets or notes.

What is job tracking software? ▾

Job tracking software is a specialised application designed to help job seekers organise and manage their job search. Job tracking apps like My Job Trackr provide tools to record job applications, track application status, schedule interviews, set reminders, and compare job offers. Job tracking software differs from basic spreadsheets by offering purpose-built features including native job search integration, automated notifications, offer comparison tools, and mobile access for managing your job search from any device.

Why use a job tracker instead of a spreadsheet? ▾

Job tracker apps like My Job Trackr offer significant advantages over spreadsheets including automated deadline reminders, integrated interview calendars, native job search capabilities, offer comparison views, and mobile access. Spreadsheets require manual updates and lack specialised job search features like Quick Import for auto-filling job details from URLs or push notifications for upcoming deadlines. A dedicated job tracker provides a purpose-built interface designed specifically for managing job applications, making it faster and more reliable than manual spreadsheet tracking.
